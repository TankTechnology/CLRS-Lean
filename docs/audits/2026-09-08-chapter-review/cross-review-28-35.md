**32-DFA-COST — uphold MAJOR.** `F32/Section_32_3_Finite_Automata.lean:125` tries suffix lengths in descending order. `transitionTable` (:664) evaluates that semantic `delta` per cell, while `transitionTableBuildCost` (:756) only sums row lengths. Thus the output-cell count does not justify the advertised construction bound (:761). `dfaMatcherCost := T.length` (:651) is also detached. However the docs explicitly call the update O(1) (:302), the returned quantity deterministic work performed (:519), and the refinement a “genuine O(1) window update” (:555). Without treating whole exponentiation as a unit primitive, repeated powers are not a constant arithmetic update. The claim needs either a precomputed modular high power and counted initialization, or an explicit abstract-slide metric that does not call the bound primitive runtime. List reconstruction and absolute-text confirmation traversals are additional literal-evaluation omissions, but should not drive severity when a list/array abstraction is intended. No actual wall-clock or formal asymptotic lower bound was proved here. Preserve the valid recurrence identity, hit-count equation, and all-occurrence correctness.
